---
title: 'Dashboard Alerts'
api: 'GET /dashboard/alerts'
---

Retrieves prioritized system alerts for the aeronautical system grouped by criticality level.

Alerts cover four categories of operational risk:
- **Expired documents** (`critical`): Staff documents with expiration date before current date (up to 10 records)
- **Expiring documents** (`warning`): Documents about to expire within configured window (default: 30 days, up to 10 records)
- **Upcoming trainings** (`info`): Active trainings scheduled within configured window (default: 7 days, up to 10 records)
- **Aircraft parts at limit** (`critical`/`warning`): Parts in OVERDUE, NEAR_LIMIT or APPROACHING_LIMIT status (up to 10 records)

Alerts are sorted by priority: `critical` > `warning` > `info`. Response is limited to maximum 20 alerts total.

## Authorization

Requires authentication via JWT cookie and statistics permissions (`requireStats`).

## Query Parameters

<ParamField query="doc_warning_days" type="integer" default="30">
  Days in advance to alert about expiring documents. Minimum value: 1.
  
  If not provided, defaults to 30 days.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="training_warning_days" type="integer" default="7">
  Days in advance to alert about upcoming trainings. Minimum value: 1.
  
  If not provided, defaults to 7 days.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="alerts" type="array">
  Array of alert objects sorted by priority (critical, warning, info)
  
  <ResponseField name="id" type="string">
    Unique identifier for the alert (e.g., `expired-doc-123`, `training-45`, `part-789`)
  </ResponseField>
  
  <ResponseField name="level" type="string">
    Alert criticality level: `critical`, `warning`, or `info`
  </ResponseField>
  
  <ResponseField name="title" type="string">
    Brief title describing the alert type
  </ResponseField>
  
  <ResponseField name="description" type="string">
    Detailed description including relevant entity information
  </ResponseField>
  
  <ResponseField name="timestamp" type="string">
    ISO 8601 timestamp (expiration date for documents/trainings, current time for parts)
  </ResponseField>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="total" type="integer">
  Total number of alerts returned (max 20)
</ResponseField>

## Alert Categories

### Critical Alerts

**Expired Documents**
- Documents with `expiration_date < current_date`
- Status must be `ACTIVE`
- Limited to 10 most urgent (oldest expiration)
- Format: `{userName} - {documentType}`

**Overdue Aircraft Parts**
- Parts with status `OVERDUE`
- Exceeded operational or calendar limits
- Limited to 10 records
- Format: `{partNumber} - {aircraftName} ({registration})`

### Warning Alerts

**Expiring Documents**
- Documents expiring within `doc_warning_days` (default: 30)
- Status must be `ACTIVE`
- Limited to 10 soonest to expire
- Format: `{userName} - {documentType}`

**Aircraft Parts Near Limit**
- Parts with status `NEAR_LIMIT` or `APPROACHING_LIMIT`
- Close to operational or calendar limits
- Limited to 10 records
- Format: `{partNumber} - {aircraftName} ({registration})`

### Info Alerts

**Upcoming Trainings**
- Trainings scheduled within `training_warning_days` (default: 7)
- Status must be `ACTIVE`
- Limited to 10 soonest scheduled
- Format: `{trainingName} - {programDate}`

## Notes

- Maximum 20 alerts returned across all categories
- Alerts are pre-sorted by priority level
- Timestamps reflect expiration/scheduled dates (not current time) for documents and trainings
- Part alerts use current timestamp as they represent real-time status
