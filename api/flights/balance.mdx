## Create Flight Balance

<Card title="POST /flight-balances" icon="plus">

</Card>

## Understanding Weight & Balance

### Key Concepts

**Center of Gravity (CG)**: The point where the aircraft would balance if suspended. Must be within manufacturer-specified limits for safe flight.

**Moment**: The product of weight and arm (distance from datum). Used to calculate CG position.

**MAC (Mean Aerodynamic Chord)**: The average chord of the wing, expressed as a percentage. CG limits are often specified as % MAC.

### Calculation Flow

1. **Operating Empty Weight**: Base aircraft weight without fuel or payload
2. **Operating with Fuel**: Add fuel weight and recalculate CG
3. **Takeoff**: Add all passengers, cargo, and calculate final takeoff CG
4. **Landing**: Account for fuel burn to verify landing weight and CG are within limits

### Safety Considerations

Weight and balance calculations are critical for:
- Ensuring the aircraft operates within structural limits
- Maintaining controllability throughout the flight
- Optimizing aircraft performance
- Meeting regulatory requirements
