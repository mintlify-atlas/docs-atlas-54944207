## Aircraft Registry

### Registering a New Aircraft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Aircraft Management">
    Navigate to the Dispatch module and select Aircraft Management from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Aircraft">
    Click the "Add Aircraft" button to open the registration form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Basic Information">
    Fill in the aircraft details:
    - Aircraft name and registration number
    - Category (Rotary Wing or Fixed Wing)
    - Manufacturer and model
    - Maximum passenger capacity
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Engine Parameters">
    Set the engine specifications:
    - Number of engines (1 or 2)
    - Time limits for each engine (in hours)
    - Cycle limits for each engine
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Weight and Balance">
    Enter the aircraft weight and balance data:
    - Empty weight
    - Center of gravity (CG) empty
    - Moment empty
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initialize Flight Hours">
    Record the current operational state:
    - Current flight time (total accumulated hours)
    - Current cycles (total accumulated cycles)
    - Total lifetime hours available
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Aircraft">
    Review all information and save the aircraft to add it to your fleet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aircraft Status Management

Each aircraft can have one of the following statuses:

- **Active**: Aircraft is operational and available for missions
- **In Maintenance**: Aircraft is undergoing scheduled or unscheduled maintenance
- **Out of Service**: Aircraft is temporarily unavailable
- **Eliminated**: Aircraft has been removed from the fleet (soft deleted)

### Changing Aircraft Status

To update an aircraft's status:

1. Locate the aircraft in the fleet list
2. Click the actions menu (three dots)
3. Select "Change Status"
4. Choose the new status from the dropdown
5. Add any relevant notes or comments
6. Confirm the status change

## Flight Hours and Maintenance Tracking

### Understanding Flight Hours

The system automatically tracks several key metrics for each aircraft:

- **Current Flight Time**: Total accumulated flight hours
- **Available Time**: Remaining hours before next major maintenance
- **Maintenance Due Percentage**: Visual indicator of maintenance proximity
- **Total Lifetime Hours**: Maximum hours available before major overhaul

<Note>
The maintenance due percentage is calculated based on the hours accumulated since the last major maintenance relative to the total hours available before the next maintenance is due.
</Note>

### Viewing Flight History

Access detailed flight history for any aircraft:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Aircraft">
    Click on an aircraft from the fleet list to open its detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Flight Records">
    Select the "Flight Records" tab to view the complete flight history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Flight Data">
    The flight records table displays:
    - Date and time of each flight
    - Flight duration and cycles
    - Origin and destination
    - Pilot in command and crew
    - Fuel consumption
    - Cumulative hours and cycles
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Parts and Components Tracking

### Managing Aircraft Parts

Track critical aircraft components and their lifecycles:

- **Part Identification**: Serial numbers and part numbers
- **Installation Date**: When the part was installed
- **Time Limits**: Hours or cycles before replacement
- **Current Status**: Serviceable, due for replacement, or expired
- **Documentation**: Certificates and compliance records

### Adding a New Part

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Aircraft Details">
    Navigate to the specific aircraft in the fleet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Parts Inventory">
    Click on the "Parts & Components" tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add New Part">
    Click "Add Part" and fill in the required information:
    - Part name and description
    - Serial number and part number
    - Installation date
    - Time or cycle limits
    - Associated documentation
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Part Record">
    Confirm the information and save the part to the aircraft inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aircraft Documentation

### Document Types

Manage various types of aircraft documentation:

- **Registration Certificates**: Aircraft registration and airworthiness certificates
- **Insurance Documents**: Proof of insurance coverage
- **Maintenance Records**: Service logs and inspection reports
- **Weight & Balance**: Official weight and balance documentation
- **Operating Manuals**: Aircraft flight manuals and checklists

### Uploading Documents

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Documents">
    Open the aircraft details and select the "Documents" tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload File">
    Click "Upload Document" and select the file from your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Categorize Document">
    Select the document type and add a description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiration">
    If applicable, set an expiration date for automatic alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Document">
    Confirm the upload to add the document to the aircraft record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Ensure all required documentation is current and uploaded to maintain compliance with aviation regulations.
</Warning>

## Fleet Dashboard

The Fleet Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of your entire aircraft fleet:

### Key Metrics

- **Total Aircraft**: Number of aircraft in your fleet
- **Operational Status**: Breakdown by aircraft status
- **Maintenance Alerts**: Aircraft approaching maintenance due dates
- **Flight Hours Summary**: Total fleet hours and utilization rates

### Filtering and Searching

Quickly find specific aircraft using:

- **Search**: Filter by registration, name, or manufacturer
- **Status Filter**: View only aircraft with specific statuses
- **Category Filter**: Filter by rotary wing or fixed wing
- **Sort Options**: Sort by registration, hours, or maintenance due date
