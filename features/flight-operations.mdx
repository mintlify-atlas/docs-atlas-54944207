## Transfer Types

SIGSAHO supports two main categories of flight operations:

### General Transfers

General transfers include non-medical operations such as:

- **Security Operations**: Law enforcement and public safety missions
- **Civil Protection**: Emergency response and disaster relief
- **Maintenance Flights**: Ferry flights and positioning
- **Administrative**: Personnel transport and logistics

### Medical Transfers

Medical transfers follow a two-phase workflow:

**Phase 1 - Medical Request**: Clinical staff creates the transfer request
**Phase 2 - Operational Dispatch**: Aviation staff assigns resources and completes the mission

## Recording a General Transfer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Transfer Registration">
    Navigate to the Services module and select "Register Transfer" from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Transfer Type">
    Choose "General Transfer" and select the specific category (Security, Civil Protection, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Mission Details">
    Fill in the core flight information:
    - Transfer date and time
    - Origin and destination (using geographic cascade selectors)
    - Requesting entity or person
    - Mission description
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Aircraft and Crew">
    Select the aircraft and crew members:
    - Choose the assigned aircraft from your fleet
    - Select the pilot in command
    - Add additional crew members as needed
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Flight Data">
    Enter operational details:
    - Flight time (in minutes)
    - Fuel consumed
    - Flight route
    - Travel time for the route
    - Additional passengers (if any)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Logbook Information">
    Record the logbook folio number for official records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Transfer">
    Review all information and submit the transfer record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Medical Transfer Workflow

Medical transfers use a bifurcated workflow to separate clinical and operational responsibilities.

### Phase 1: Medical Request

Clinical staff initiates the medical transfer:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Medical Request">
    Navigate to Medical Transfers and click "New Request".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Medical Details">
    Fill in the medical information:
    - Hospital of origin
    - Destination hospital
    - Medical transfer type (Emergency, Scheduled, etc.)
    - Diagnosis type and details
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Patient Information">
    Register patient details (encrypted for privacy):
    - Patient name and identification
    - Age and vital information
    - Medical condition
    - Special requirements
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Origin and Destination">
    Use the geographic selectors to specify:
    - Origin location (state, region, municipality, locality)
    - Destination location
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Request">
    Save the medical request. Status is set to "In Process" pending operational completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Patient data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption to ensure HIPAA-level privacy compliance.
</Note>

### Phase 2: Operational Completion

Aviation staff completes the mission with operational details:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Pending Mission">
    From the Medical Transfers list, select a mission with "In Process" status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Completion Form">
    Click "Complete Mission" to open the operational dispatch form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Resources">
    Assign operational resources:
    - Aircraft selection
    - Crew members (pilot, co-pilot, medical staff)
    - Medical equipment used
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Flight Details">
    Enter flight operational data:
    - Actual flight time
    - Fuel consumption
    - Departure and arrival times
    - Route flown
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize Mission">
    Submit the completion form. The system:
    - Updates aircraft flight hours and cycles
    - Changes transfer status to "Completed"
    - Generates mission summary report
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Weight and Balance Calculations

Before every flight, pilots must calculate and verify weight and balance to ensure safe operations.

### Creating a Weight and Balance Sheet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Weight & Balance">
    From the Air Traffic Control module, select "Weight & Balance".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Aircraft">
    Choose the aircraft for which you're calculating weight and balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Basic Empty Weight">
    The system automatically populates:
    - Operating empty weight
    - Empty CG (center of gravity)
    - Empty moment
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Fuel Load">
    Enter fuel quantities:
    - Fuel weight
    - Fuel arm (distance from datum)
    - System calculates moment automatically
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Crew and Passengers">
    For each crew member and passenger:
    - Enter weight
    - Specify seating position (arm)
    - System calculates individual moments
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Cargo">
    If carrying cargo:
    - Enter cargo weight
    - Specify cargo position
    - Add cargo moment
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Calculations">
    The system automatically calculates:
    - **Takeoff Weight**: Total weight at departure
    - **Takeoff CG**: Center of gravity at takeoff
    - **Takeoff MAC**: Mean Aerodynamic Chord percentage
    - **Landing Weight**: Estimated weight after fuel burn
    - **Landing CG**: Center of gravity at landing
    - **Landing MAC**: MAC percentage at landing
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Limits">
    Ensure all values are within the aircraft's operational limits:
    - Maximum takeoff weight
    - CG envelope
    - Maximum landing weight
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Print">
    Save the weight and balance sheet and print for flight documentation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Never depart if the calculated weight and balance falls outside the approved aircraft envelope. Recalculate with adjusted loading.
</Warning>

## Crew Management in Flight Operations

### Assigning Crew Members

The system supports multiple crew roles:

- **Pilot in Command**: Primary flight authority
- **First Officer**: Co-pilot or second-in-command
- **Flight Engineer**: For aircraft requiring flight engineers
- **Medical Personnel**: Doctors, nurses, paramedics (for medical transfers)
- **Additional Crew**: Other operational personnel

### Crew Assignment Process

When recording a transfer:

1. **Select Crew Members**: Use the multi-select dropdown to choose crew
2. **Assign Roles**: Designate the specific role for each crew member
3. **Verify Qualifications**: System highlights if crew lacks required certifications
4. **Track Participation**: Each flight is logged to the crew member's flight history

## Transfer Status Tracking

Transfers move through several status states:

- **In Process**: Transfer created but not yet completed
- **Completed**: Flight successfully completed and logged
- **Cancelled**: Transfer cancelled before execution
- **Pending**: Awaiting additional information or approval

### Changing Transfer Status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate Transfer">
    Find the transfer in the transfers list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actions Menu">
    Click the actions menu (three dots) on the transfer row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Status Change">
    Choose "Change Status" from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose New Status">
    Select the new status from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Notes">
    Enter any relevant notes or reasons for the status change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Save the status change to update the transfer record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Flight Statistics and Reporting

The system automatically generates statistics from flight operations:

### Available Reports

- **Flight Hours by Aircraft**: Total hours per aircraft over a date range
- **Transfer Type Analysis**: Breakdown of missions by category
- **Crew Utilization**: Flight hours per crew member
- **Medical Transfer Statistics**: Medical mission metrics and outcomes
- **Fuel Consumption**: Fuel usage trends and analysis

### Generating Reports

1. Navigate to Statistics module
2. Select the report type
3. Set date range filters
4. Choose additional filters (aircraft, crew, transfer type)
5. Generate and export report (PDF, Excel, CSV)

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Accurate Time Recording">
    Always record actual flight times accurately. This is critical for maintenance scheduling, crew duty time tracking, and operational statistics.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Weight & Balance Before Every Flight">
    Complete a weight and balance calculation for every flight, even if the loading seems similar to previous flights. Conditions change.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Crew Qualifications">
    Verify that all assigned crew members have current certifications and medical clearances before dispatch.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Timely Documentation">
    Record transfers as soon as possible after completion while details are fresh. This ensures accuracy and compliance.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Medical Privacy">
    Handle all medical transfer data with strict confidentiality. Only authorized personnel should access patient information.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
