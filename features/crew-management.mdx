## Personnel Types

The system supports various types of aviation personnel:

- **Pilots**: Captains, first officers, and flight instructors
- **Medical Personnel**: Flight doctors, nurses, and paramedics
- **Maintenance Staff**: Aircraft mechanics and inspectors
- **Support Staff**: Dispatchers, operations coordinators
- **Administrative**: Management and oversight personnel

## Adding New Personnel

Add new crew members using the multi-step personnel wizard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Personnel Management">
    Navigate to the Tracking module and select "Personnel" from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start New Personnel Wizard">
    Click "Add Personnel" to open the creation wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 1: General Information">
    Enter basic personnel data:
    - Full name (first name, last name, second last name)
    - Email address (must be unique)
    - Phone number
    - Date of birth
    - Weight (for weight and balance calculations)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Position and Roles">
    Select the crew member's:
    - Position (Pilot, Medical Staff, Mechanic, etc.)
    - System roles (determines access permissions)
    - License number (if applicable)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Login Access">
    Choose whether this person will have system login access:
    - If yes, create username and password
    - If no, they'll appear in crew selection but cannot log in
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2: Documentation">
    Upload required documentation:
    - Professional licenses
    - Medical certificates
    - Insurance documents
    - Identification documents
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Document Expiration Dates">
    For each document, set the expiration date to enable automatic alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3: Training Assignment">
    Assign initial training courses:
    - Select applicable training programs
    - Set target completion dates
    - Mark any pre-completed certifications
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Save">
    Review all information and save to create the personnel record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Personnel Profiles

Each crew member has a comprehensive profile with multiple sections:

### Profile Overview

The main profile displays:

- **Header Information**: Name, position, status, and contact details
- **Quick Stats**: Active documents, pending trainings, flight hours
- **Status Badge**: Visual indicator of overall compliance status

### Documents Tab

View and manage all personnel documentation:

- **License Documents**: Professional licenses and certifications
- **Medical Certificates**: Current medical clearance status
- **Training Certificates**: Completed training documentation
- **Other Documents**: Insurance, identification, and miscellaneous files

**Document Management Actions:**
- Upload new documents
- Download existing documents
- Set/update expiration dates
- Delete obsolete documents
- View document history

### Training Tab

Track all training and certification activities:

- **Active Trainings**: Currently in-progress courses
- **Completed Trainings**: Historical certification records
- **Upcoming Requirements**: Scheduled or required future training
- **Certification Status**: Overall training compliance status

### Flight History Tab

For flight crew, view complete flight participation:

- Date and time of each flight
- Aircraft flown
- Role in the flight (Pilot in Command, First Officer, etc.)
- Flight duration
- Cumulative flight hours
- Recent flight activity

## Training and Certifications

### Creating a Training Program

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Training Management">
    Navigate to Tracking > Capacitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Training">
    Click "Create Training Program" to open the training form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Training Details">
    Fill in the program information:
    - Training name and code
    - Description and objectives
    - Duration (hours or days)
    - Training type (Initial, Recurrent, Specialized)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Requirements">
    Define training requirements:
    - Applicable roles (Pilot, Medical, Maintenance)
    - Prerequisites
    - Recurrency period (if applicable)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Training Materials">
    Upload course materials:
    - Training manuals
    - Presentation slides
    - Reference documents
    - Exam materials
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Training Program">
    Save the training to make it available for assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Assigning Training to Personnel

There are two ways to assign training:

**Option 1: From the Training Program**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Training Program">
    Navigate to the specific training program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Users">
    Click "Assign Users" to open the assignment dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Personnel">
    Choose one or more personnel to assign this training.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Dates">
    Define the start date and target completion date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Assignment">
    Save to assign the training to the selected personnel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Option 2: From the Personnel Profile**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Personnel Profile">
    Navigate to the crew member's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Training Tab">
    Select the "Training" tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Training">
    Click "Assign Training" and select from available programs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Parameters">
    Set start and completion dates for the training.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Save the assignment to add it to the person's training record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Completing Training

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate Training Record">
    Find the in-progress training in the personnel's training tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Training">
    Click "Complete Training" to open the completion dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Completion Details">
    Fill in the completion information:
    - Actual completion date
    - Final score or grade (if applicable)
    - Instructor name
    - Notes or comments
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Certificate">
    Upload the official training certificate or completion document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Completion">
    Save to mark the training as completed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
For recurrent training, the system will automatically calculate and schedule the next required training date based on the recurrency period.
</Note>

## Document Expiration Management

### Setting Up Automatic Alerts

The system can automatically alert you when documents are approaching expiration:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Alert Settings">
    Navigate to Tracking > Alerts > Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Alert Thresholds">
    Set the advance warning periods:
    - Days before expiration to send first warning (e.g., 90 days)
    - Days before expiration to send urgent warning (e.g., 30 days)
    - Days before expiration to send critical warning (e.g., 7 days)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Notification Methods">
    Choose how alerts are delivered:
    - In-system notifications
    - Email notifications
    - SMS notifications (if configured)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Recipients">
    Specify who receives alerts:
    - The crew member themselves
    - Their direct supervisor
    - Administrative personnel
    - Operations managers
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Settings">
    Confirm the alert configuration to activate automatic monitoring.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Viewing Alerts

Access the Alerts Dashboard to see all current alerts:

- **Critical Alerts**: Documents expired or expiring within 7 days (red)
- **Urgent Alerts**: Documents expiring within 30 days (orange)
- **Warning Alerts**: Documents expiring within 90 days (yellow)
- **All Good**: All documents current (green)

### Responding to Alerts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Alert Details">
    Click on an alert to see the affected personnel and documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Personnel">
    Use the "Send Reminder" feature to notify the crew member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Renewed Document">
    Once the document is renewed, upload the new version with updated expiration date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dismiss Alert">
    The alert automatically clears when the document is updated with a future expiration date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Personnel Status Management

Crew members can have different operational statuses:

- **Active**: Currently operational and available for assignment
- **On Leave**: Temporarily unavailable (vacation, personal leave)
- **Suspended**: Temporarily grounded pending investigation or renewal
- **Inactive**: No longer actively flying or working
- **Eliminated**: Removed from the system (soft deleted)

### Changing Personnel Status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Personnel Profile">
    Navigate to the crew member's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change Status">
    Click the "Change Status" button in the profile header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select New Status">
    Choose the new status from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Reason">
    Enter the reason for the status change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Effective Date">
    Specify when the status change takes effect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Change">
    Save the status change to update the personnel record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Changing a crew member's status to Suspended or Inactive will prevent them from being assigned to future flights.
</Warning>

## Role-Based Access Control

The system implements granular permissions based on user roles:

### Available Roles

- **Admin**: Full system access, can manage all personnel and settings
- **Director**: Read-only access to all data, cannot modify personnel
- **Medical Editor**: Can manage medical transfers and medical personnel
- **Operations Editor**: Can manage flight operations and crew assignments
- **Dispatcher**: Can create transfers and assign resources
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to assigned areas

### Assigning Roles

When creating or editing personnel:

1. Navigate to the role assignment section
2. Select one or more applicable roles
3. The system automatically grants appropriate permissions
4. Personnel can only access features allowed by their assigned roles

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Regular Document Reviews">
    Schedule monthly reviews of document expiration dates to ensure no certifications lapse unexpectedly. Don't rely solely on automatic alerts.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Training Ahead of Schedule">
    Schedule recurrent training to complete at least 30 days before the current certification expires to allow buffer time.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Complete Profiles">
    Ensure all personnel profiles are 100% complete with all required documentation uploaded and expiration dates set.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Accurate Contact Information">
    Keep email addresses and phone numbers current so crew members receive alerts about their own document expirations.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Document Retention">
    Even when uploading renewed documents, keep historical versions for audit trail and compliance purposes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reporting and Analytics

### Available Personnel Reports

- **Crew Qualifications Summary**: Overview of all crew qualifications and currencies
- **Training Compliance**: Percentage of personnel current on required training
- **Document Expiration Forecast**: Upcoming expirations over the next 6-12 months
- **Flight Hours by Personnel**: Individual and aggregate flight time statistics
- **Training Completion Rates**: Training program effectiveness metrics

### Generating Reports

1. Navigate to Reports > Personnel Reports
2. Select the report type
3. Set filters (date range, personnel type, status)
4. Generate report
5. Export in desired format (PDF, Excel, CSV)
