## Service Types

SIGSAHO tracks various types of aircraft services:

### Preventive Maintenance

- **100-Hour Inspections**: Required every 100 flight hours for commercial operations
- **Annual Inspections**: Calendar-based yearly inspections
- **Progressive Inspections**: Phased inspections for high-utilization aircraft
- **Preflight Inspections**: Daily or pre-flight checks

### Scheduled Maintenance

- **Phase Checks**: A-check, B-check, C-check maintenance phases
- **Engine Overhauls**: Time-based or condition-based engine work
- **Component Replacements**: Life-limited parts reaching time limits
- **System Servicing**: Hydraulic, fuel, electrical system services

### Unscheduled Maintenance

- **Repairs**: Discrepancy repairs and troubleshooting
- **Modifications**: Aircraft modifications and upgrades
- **Airworthiness Directives**: Mandatory AD compliance
- **Service Bulletins**: Optional or recommended manufacturer bulletins

### Special Inspections

- **Hard Landing Inspections**: After exceeding landing limits
- **Lightning Strike Inspections**: After lightning encounters
- **Bird Strike Inspections**: After bird strike incidents
- **Foreign Object Damage**: FOD inspections and repairs

## Creating a Service Record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Service Tracking">
    Navigate to the Service Tracking module from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Service">
    Click "Schedule Service" to open the service creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Aircraft">
    Choose the aircraft that requires service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Service Type">
    Select the type of service from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Service Description">
    Provide a detailed description of the service:
    - What work needs to be performed
    - Regulatory reference (if applicable)
    - Special tools or parts required
    - Estimated duration
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Service Schedule">
    Define when the service is due using one or both criteria:
    - **Calendar Date**: Specific date when service is due
    - **Flight Hours**: Aircraft hours when service is due
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Current Hours">
    The system captures aircraft hours at service creation for tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Notifications">
    Set up automatic alerts:
    - Email addresses to notify
    - Phone numbers for SMS alerts
    - Days or hours before due to send reminders
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Service Record">
    Save the service. It appears in the services list with status "Pending."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
For recurring services, the system can automatically create the next service record when the current one is completed.
</Note>

## Service Scheduling Methods

Services can be scheduled using different criteria:

### Calendar-Based Scheduling

Services due on specific calendar dates:

- Annual inspections (e.g., due every 12 months)
- Certificate renewals
- Periodic system checks
- Regulatory compliance deadlines

**Example**: Annual inspection due on December 31, 2026

### Hour-Based Scheduling

Services triggered by flight hours:

- 100-hour inspections
- Engine time-based overhauls
- Component replacement intervals
- Phase maintenance checks

**Example**: 100-hour inspection due at 1250.0 flight hours

### Dual-Criteria Scheduling

Services due based on whichever comes first:

- Calendar date OR flight hours
- Ensures service isn't missed due to low utilization or high usage
- Common for critical inspections

**Example**: Inspection due on June 30, 2026 OR at 2000.0 hours (whichever occurs first)

## Service Status Workflow

Services progress through several status states:

### Pending

Service is scheduled but not yet due:

- Appears in upcoming services list
- Notifications not yet active
- Aircraft remains available for operations
- Can be edited or rescheduled

### Due

Service criteria have been met:

- Calendar date has arrived, or
- Aircraft has reached specified flight hours
- Notifications automatically sent to configured recipients
- Aircraft should be scheduled for maintenance soon

### In Progress

Maintenance work has begun:

- Aircraft status may be set to "In Maintenance"
- Work is actively being performed
- Parts may be ordered or consumed
- Technicians assigned

### Completed

Service has been finished and signed off:

- All work completed
- Documentation uploaded
- Aircraft returned to service
- Recurring services auto-schedule next occurrence

### Cancelled

Service was cancelled or no longer required:

- Service not performed
- Reason for cancellation documented
- Does not affect aircraft status
- Retained in history for audit trail

## Completing a Service

When maintenance work is finished:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate Service Record">
    Find the service in the Services and Inspections table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Service Details">
    Click on the service to view full information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Service">
    Click "Complete Service" to open the completion form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Completion Date">
    Record when the service was actually completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Work Performed">
    Provide detailed completion information:
    - Summary of work performed
    - Discrepancies found and resolved
    - Parts replaced
    - Technician names and certificate numbers
    - Hours spent on the work
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Parts Used">
    Link parts from inventory:
    - Parts removed (if applicable)
    - Parts installed
    - Serial numbers and part numbers
    - Update inventory quantities
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Documentation">
    Attach service documentation:
    - Work orders
    - Maintenance logs
    - Sign-off sheets
    - Test results
    - Parts certificates (FAA Form 8130-3, etc.)
    - Photos (if applicable)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Aircraft Hours">
    If needed, update aircraft flight hours to current reading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Service Authorization">
    If aircraft was grounded:
    - Verify all work is complete
    - Confirm aircraft is airworthy
    - Change aircraft status to "Active"
    - Document return-to-service authorization
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Completion">
    Finalize the service record. The system:
    - Updates service status to "Completed"
    - Updates aircraft maintenance history
    - Recalculates maintenance due dates
    - Creates next occurrence (if recurring)
    - Archives all documentation
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
All completed services must include proper documentation and sign-off. Undocumented maintenance cannot legally return an aircraft to service.
</Warning>

## Service Notifications

Automated notifications help prevent missed maintenance:

### Notification Triggers

Notifications are automatically sent when:

- **Service Approaching**: Configurable days/hours before due
- **Service Due**: When service criteria are met
- **Service Overdue**: If service is not completed by due date
- **Service Completed**: Confirmation when work is finished
- **Service Cancelled**: If service is cancelled

### Notification Configuration

For each service, configure:

**Recipients**
- Email addresses (multiple allowed)
- Phone numbers for SMS
- Role-based recipients (e.g., all dispatchers)

**Timing**
- Days before calendar due date
- Flight hours before hour-based due
- Escalation intervals for overdue services

**Content**
- Service description
- Aircraft identification
- Due date or hours
- Priority level
- Link to service record

### Managing Notification Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Service Record">
    Navigate to the service you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Notifications">
    Click "Edit Notifications" or access during service creation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Email Recipients">
    Enter email addresses, separated by commas or one per line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add SMS Recipients">
    Enter phone numbers in international format (e.g., +52-xxx-xxx-xxxx).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Reminder Timing">
    Configure when notifications are sent:
    - For calendar-based: Days before due (e.g., 30, 14, 7 days)
    - For hour-based: Hours before due (e.g., 50, 25, 10 hours)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Configuration">
    Save the notification settings to activate alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Parts Integration

Service records integrate with parts inventory:

### Linking Parts to Services

When completing a service:

1. **Parts Removed**: Document removed parts
   - Part number and serial number
   - Reason for removal
   - Time/cycles at removal
   - Disposition (scrap, repair, bench stock)

2. **Parts Installed**: Record installed parts
   - Part number and serial number
   - Manufacturer and certification
   - Installation date and aircraft hours
   - Expected life limits

3. **Inventory Updates**: System automatically:
   - Reduces inventory quantity for installed parts
   - Updates part status (Installed, Removed, etc.)
   - Creates part lifecycle tracking
   - Schedules next replacement if life-limited

### Part Traceability

The system maintains complete part traceability:

- Which service installed the part
- Aircraft hours at installation
- Time/cycles accumulated on part
- Next inspection or replacement due
- Complete chain of custody

## Regulatory Compliance

Ensure compliance with aviation regulations:

### Airworthiness Directives (ADs)

Track compliance with mandatory ADs:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create AD Service Record">
    When an AD is issued, create a service record for compliance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter AD Details">
    Document:
    - AD number and title
    - Effective date
    - Compliance method
    - Recurring or one-time
    - Compliance deadline
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Compliance">
    Monitor the service until completion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Completion">
    When complied with, complete the service record with:
    - Method of compliance
    - Date of compliance
    - Aircraft hours at compliance
    - Supporting documentation
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recurring ADs">
    If recurring, the system auto-schedules the next occurrence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Service Bulletins

Track optional manufacturer service bulletins:

- Record SB number and description
- Mark as mandatory or optional
- Schedule compliance if implementing
- Document decision if not implementing

### Inspection Programs

Manage inspection program compliance:

- Track which inspection program applies (100-hour, progressive, etc.)
- Ensure inspections are completed within required intervals
- Maintain records of all inspections
- Document any program changes or approvals

## Service Analytics

Gain insights from maintenance data:

### Available Metrics

- **Maintenance Cost per Hour**: Total maintenance spend divided by flight hours
- **Mean Time Between Failures**: Reliability metrics
- **On-Time Completion Rate**: Percentage of services completed by due date
- **Aircraft Downtime**: Hours/days aircraft unavailable due to maintenance
- **Parts Consumption Trends**: Which parts are most frequently replaced

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Schedule Maintenance in Advance">
    Don't wait until services are due. Schedule maintenance windows at least 30 days in advance to ensure aircraft availability and parts procurement.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Complete Documentation">
    Always upload complete documentation for every service. Include work orders, logbook entries, parts certificates, and sign-offs. Incomplete records can ground aircraft during audits.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Track Part Serial Numbers">
    For all serialized parts, maintain complete records of serial numbers, installation dates, and time limits. This is critical for airworthiness.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Use Recurring Services">
    For regular inspections (100-hour, annual), set up recurring services so the system automatically creates the next service when the current one is completed.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Monitor Service Trends">
    Review service analytics regularly to identify recurring issues or opportunities to improve maintenance efficiency and reduce costs.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Verify Technician Qualifications">
    Always verify that technicians performing maintenance hold appropriate licenses and ratings for the work being performed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Service Not Showing as Due">
    If a service isn't triggering as due:
    - Verify the due date or hours are set correctly
    - Check that aircraft current hours are updating after flights
    - Ensure the service status is "Pending" not "Cancelled"
    - Review notification configuration
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Cannot Complete Service">
    If unable to complete a service:
    - Ensure you have appropriate permissions (dispatcher or admin role)
    - Verify all required fields are filled in
    - Check that completion date is not in the future
    - Confirm supporting documentation is attached
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Notifications Not Being Sent">
    If notifications aren't working:
    - Verify email addresses are correct and not typos
    - Check spam/junk folders
    - Ensure notification timing is configured
    - Confirm system email settings are correct (contact administrator)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
