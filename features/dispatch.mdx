## Aircraft Dispatch Dashboard

The dispatch dashboard provides real-time fleet status and operational readiness.

### Dashboard Components

**Fleet Status Cards**
- Total aircraft count
- Aircraft available for dispatch
- Aircraft in maintenance
- Aircraft out of service

**Key Performance Indicators**
- Total fleet flight hours
- Average aircraft utilization
- Maintenance compliance percentage
- Upcoming service requirements

**Quick Actions**
- Dispatch aircraft to mission
- Record flight completion
- Schedule maintenance
- Update aircraft status

### Viewing Aircraft Status

The dispatch table displays all aircraft with critical information:

- **Registration**: Aircraft registration number
- **Aircraft Name**: Friendly identifier
- **Status**: Current operational status
- **Available Hours**: Flight hours remaining before maintenance
- **Current Hours**: Total accumulated flight time
- **Last Flight**: Date of most recent flight
- **Maintenance Due**: Percentage toward next maintenance
- **Actions**: Quick access to operations

### Filtering the Fleet

Quickly find aircraft using filters:

- **Status Filter**: View only Active, In Maintenance, or Out of Service aircraft
- **Category Filter**: Filter by Rotary Wing or Fixed Wing
- **Availability Filter**: Show only aircraft available for dispatch
- **Search**: Search by registration or name
- **Sort**: Sort by any column (hours, maintenance due, etc.)

## Dispatching Aircraft

### Pre-Dispatch Checklist

Before dispatching an aircraft, verify:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Aircraft Status">
    Confirm the aircraft status is "Active" and available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flight Hours">
    Check that sufficient flight hours are available before maintenance is due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crew Availability">
    Verify qualified crew members are available and current on certifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weather Conditions">
    Review weather at origin, destination, and along the route.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weight and Balance">
    Calculate or verify weight and balance for the mission loading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Documentation">
    Ensure all required aircraft documents are current and onboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Dispatch Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Aircraft">
    From the dispatch dashboard, select the aircraft to dispatch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Mission">
    Link the aircraft to a flight transfer or mission:
    - Select from existing pending transfers
    - Or create a new transfer record
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Crew">
    Select the crew members for the mission:
    - Pilot in Command (required)
    - First Officer (if applicable)
    - Additional crew as needed
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Readiness">
    Final checks before dispatch:
    - Weight and balance completed
    - Weather briefing obtained
    - All crew qualified and current
    - Aircraft inspected and fueled
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Dispatch">
    Click "Dispatch" to authorize the aircraft for the mission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Dispatch authorization updates the aircraft status and creates an audit trail for operational tracking.
</Note>

## Flight Records Management

Maintain comprehensive flight records and logbooks for each aircraft.

### Flight Records Table

The flight records section displays detailed flight history:

**Flight Information**
- Flight date and time
- Flight number or reference
- Origin and destination
- Flight time (hours and minutes)

**Engine and Cycle Data**
- Engine hours (for each engine)
- Cycles completed
- Cumulative totals

**Crew Information**
- Pilot in Command
- First Officer
- Additional crew

**Operational Data**
- Fuel consumed
- Passengers carried
- Cargo weight
- Logbook folio number

### Adding a Flight Record

Manually add a flight record if not auto-created from transfer:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Flight Records">
    Navigate to the aircraft's flight records section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add New Record">
    Click "Add Flight Record" to open the entry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Flight Details">
    Fill in all required flight information:
    - Date and time of flight
    - Origin and destination
    - Flight time (in decimal hours or hours:minutes)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Engine Data">
    Enter engine-specific information:
    - Hours for each engine
    - Cycles completed
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Crew Information">
    Assign crew members who operated the flight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include Operational Data">
    Record fuel, passengers, and cargo details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Record">
    Save the flight record. The system automatically:
    - Updates aircraft total hours
    - Updates maintenance calculations
    - Adds to crew member flight logs
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Flight Hours Calculation

The system automatically maintains cumulative flight data:

- **Individual Flight Hours**: Time for each specific flight
- **Cumulative Hours**: Running total of all flight time
- **Hours Since Maintenance**: Time accumulated since last major service
- **Available Hours**: Remaining time before next maintenance due

<Warning>
Flight hours directly impact maintenance scheduling. Always ensure flight records are accurate and entered promptly after each flight.
</Warning>

## Services and Inspections

Manage scheduled and unscheduled maintenance services.

### Service Types

The system tracks various maintenance categories:

- **Scheduled Maintenance**: Time-based or hour-based recurring services
- **Inspections**: Required regulatory inspections (100-hour, annual, etc.)
- **Repairs**: Unscheduled maintenance and repairs
- **Modifications**: Aircraft modifications and upgrades
- **Special Inspections**: Event-driven inspections (hard landing, overspeed, etc.)

### Scheduling a Service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Services">
    Navigate to Dispatch > Services and Inspections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Service">
    Click "Schedule Service" to open the service form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Aircraft">
    Choose the aircraft requiring service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Service Type">
    Select the service type and provide detailed description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Schedule">
    Schedule the service based on:
    - **Date**: Calendar date for service
    - **Flight Hours**: When aircraft reaches specific hours
    - **Both**: Whichever comes first
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Service Details">
    Include additional information:
    - Service provider or technician
    - Estimated duration
    - Required parts
    - Cost estimate
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Notifications">
    Set up automatic notifications:
    - Email addresses to notify
    - Phone numbers for SMS alerts
    - Advance notice period (e.g., 7 days before due)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Service">
    Save the service record. It appears in the services table with status "Pending."
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Service Status Workflow

Services progress through several status states:

**Pending**: Service scheduled but not yet due
**Due**: Service is now due or aircraft has reached specified hours
**In Progress**: Service work has begun
**Completed**: Service finished and signed off
**Cancelled**: Service cancelled or no longer required

### Completing a Service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate Service Record">
    Find the service in the services table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Service Details">
    Click on the service to view full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Service">
    Click "Complete Service" to open the completion form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Completion Details">
    Enter completion information:
    - Actual completion date
    - Work performed
    - Parts used
    - Technician signature
    - Discrepancies found and resolved
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Documentation">
    Attach service documentation:
    - Work orders
    - Maintenance logs
    - Parts certificates
    - Sign-off sheets
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Service">
    If service required grounding the aircraft:
    - Mark aircraft as returned to service
    - Update aircraft status to "Active"
    - Record return to service authorization
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Completion">
    Finalize the service record. The system:
    - Updates service status to "Completed"
    - Updates aircraft maintenance history
    - Recalculates maintenance due percentages
    - Archives service documentation
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Service Notifications

The system automatically sends notifications when:

- Service is approaching due date (configurable advance notice)
- Service is now due
- Service is overdue
- Service is completed

Notifications can be sent via:
- In-system notifications
- Email
- SMS (if configured)

## Parts and Inventory Management

Track aircraft parts, components, and inventory.

### Inventory Categories

- **Life-Limited Parts**: Components with time or cycle limits
- **Consumables**: Fluids, filters, and consumable items
- **Rotables**: Repairable components that rotate through service
- **Expendables**: Single-use parts
- **Tools and Equipment**: Maintenance tools and ground equipment

### Inventory Table Features

The inventory management table includes:

**Part Information**
- Part number and serial number
- Part name and description
- Manufacturer
- Category

**Status Tracking**
- Current status (Serviceable, Unserviceable, Installed, In Repair)
- Location (aircraft, warehouse, shop)
- Quantity on hand

**Lifecycle Data**
- Installation date (for installed parts)
- Time since installation
- Time/cycles remaining
- Next inspection due

**Documentation**
- Certificates of conformity
- Test reports
- Maintenance records

### Adding Inventory Items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Inventory">
    Navigate to Dispatch > Components (Inventory).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add New Part">
    Click "Add Component" to open the entry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Part Details">
    Fill in part identification:
    - Part number
    - Serial number (if serialized)
    - Part name and description
    - Manufacturer
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Category and Limits">
    Specify:
    - Part category
    - Time limits (hours or calendar)
    - Cycle limits (if applicable)
    - Shelf life (if applicable)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Quantity and Location">
    Enter:
    - Quantity on hand
    - Storage location
    - Reorder point (minimum quantity)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Certifications">
    Attach required documentation:
    - FAA 8130-3 or equivalent
    - Test reports
    - Manufacturer certificates
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Inventory Item">
    Save to add the part to inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Tracking Installed Parts

When installing a part on an aircraft:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate Part in Inventory">
    Find the part to be installed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Installation">
    Click "Install Part" and select:
    - Aircraft where installed
    - Installation date
    - Aircraft hours at installation
    - Installed by (technician)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Status">
    Part status changes to "Installed" and is linked to the aircraft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Removal Schedule">
    System calculates removal due date based on:
    - Part time limits
    - Current aircraft hours
    - Calendar limits
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Inventory Alerts

Receive automatic alerts for:

- Parts approaching time/cycle limits
- Low inventory (below reorder point)
- Expired shelf-life items
- Required inspections due
- Parts reaching calendar limits

## Operational Reports

Generate dispatch and maintenance reports:

### Available Reports

- **Fleet Status Report**: Snapshot of entire fleet operational status
- **Flight Hours Summary**: Total hours by aircraft and time period
- **Maintenance Compliance**: Percentage of services completed on time
- **Aircraft Utilization**: Usage rates and availability metrics
- **Parts Consumption**: Parts usage trends and costs
- **Crew Utilization**: Hours flown per crew member

### Generating Reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports">
    Access the Reports section from the Dispatch module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Report Type">
    Choose the report you need from the available options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Parameters">
    Define report parameters:
    - Date range
    - Aircraft filter (specific aircraft or all)
    - Additional filters (status, service type, etc.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Report">
    Click "Generate" to create the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Results">
    Review the report on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export Report">
    Export in your preferred format:
    - PDF for official records
    - Excel for data analysis
    - CSV for integration with other systems
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Daily Fleet Status Review">
    Review fleet status every morning before operations begin. Verify aircraft availability, pending maintenance, and crew readiness.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Accurate Flight Record Entry">
    Enter flight records immediately after each flight while details are fresh. Accurate records are critical for maintenance scheduling.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Proactive Maintenance Scheduling">
    Schedule maintenance services well in advance. Don't wait until services are overdue, which can ground aircraft unexpectedly.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Parts Inventory Management">
    Maintain adequate inventory of critical parts. Track usage patterns and adjust reorder points to prevent stock-outs.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Documentation Discipline">
    Always attach supporting documentation to service records. This creates a complete audit trail for compliance and resale value.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Aircraft Shows In Maintenance But No Active Service">
    Check if there's a pending service record that wasn't completed or cancelled. Update the service status to return the aircraft to active status.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Flight Hours Not Updating After Recording Flight">
    Verify that the flight record was saved successfully. Check that the flight time was entered in the correct format (decimal hours). If the issue persists, contact support.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Maintenance Percentage Showing Incorrect Value">
    The maintenance percentage is calculated based on hours since last major maintenance. Verify that:
    - Last major maintenance date is correct in aircraft profile
    - Hours before last maintenance is accurate
    - Total lifetime hours is set correctly
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
