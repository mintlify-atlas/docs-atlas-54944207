This guide walks you through the authentication process in SIGSAHO, including login procedures, role-based access control, and security best practices.

## Overview

SIGSAHO uses a secure, cookie-based authentication system with role-based access control (RBAC). Your access level determines which features and data you can view and modify.

## Logging In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the login page">
    Open your web browser and navigate to the SIGSAHO application URL. You'll be automatically redirected to the login page if you're not authenticated.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Provide your login credentials:
    - **Username or Email**: Enter either your username (minimum 3 characters) or your registered email address
    - **Password**: Enter your password (minimum 6 characters)
    
    <Note>
    The system validates your input format before submitting to the server. Invalid formats will show an immediate error message.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optional: Enable 'Remember Me'">
    Check the "Remember Me" option if you want the system to maintain your session for an extended period on this device.
    
    <Warning>
    Only use "Remember Me" on personal devices. Never enable this option on shared or public computers.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and access the system">
    Click the "Sign In" button. Upon successful authentication:
    - You'll be redirected to the Dashboard or your originally requested page
    - A secure HTTP-only cookie will be set to maintain your session
    - Your user profile and permissions will be loaded
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Common Login Errors

If you encounter issues during login, check the following:

| Error Message | Cause | Solution |
|--------------|-------|----------|
| Invalid credentials | Incorrect username/password | Verify your credentials and try again. Contact your administrator if you've forgotten your password. |
| User not authorized to login | Account lacks login permissions | Your account role doesn't have login privileges. Contact an administrator. |
| Too many requests | Rate limit exceeded | Wait a few minutes before attempting to login again. |
| Account blocked | Multiple failed attempts | Contact your system administrator to unlock your account. |

<Tip>
For security reasons, the password field is automatically cleared after a failed login attempt with a 401 error.
</Tip>

## Understanding Roles

SIGSAHO implements a role-based access control system. Your assigned role determines your permissions throughout the application.

### Available Roles

**Administrative Roles** (Full System Access)
- `ADMINISTRADOR` - Full administrative privileges
- `DIRECTOR` - Director-level access with read/write permissions

**Editorial Roles** (Content Management)
- `EDITOR-TM` - Technical maintenance editor
- `EDITOR-TG` - General editor

**Operational Roles** (Day-to-day Operations)
- `MANTENIMIENTO` - Maintenance operations
- `CAPACITACION` - Training and certification management
- `DESPACHADOR` - Dispatcher/Flight operations

**Identification Roles** (Limited Login Access)
- `MEDICO` - Medical personnel (limited access)
- `PILOTO` - Pilot (flight-specific access)
- `ENFERMERO` - Nursing staff
- `TSUP` - Technical supervisor

<Note>
Only users with Administrative, Editorial, or Operational roles can login to the web platform. Identification roles are used for record-keeping and may have restricted access to specific modules.
</Note>

## Session Security

### How Sessions Work

SIGSAHO uses a "stateless" client architecture for maximum security:

1. **No Local Storage**: Authentication tokens are never stored in browser localStorage or sessionStorage
2. **HTTP-Only Cookies**: Session data is maintained exclusively through secure HTTP-only cookies managed by the backend
3. **In-Memory Tokens**: Access tokens remain only in volatile memory, preventing XSS attacks
4. **Automatic Refresh**: Sessions are automatically validated and refreshed on page load

### Session Restoration

When you return to SIGSAHO:
- The application automatically validates your existing session cookie
- If valid, you're logged in without re-entering credentials
- If expired or invalid, you're redirected to the login page

<Tip>
The system distinguishes between authentication errors (401, 403) and temporary network issues (429, 500). You won't be logged out due to temporary server problems.
</Tip>

## Protected Routes

Certain pages and features are protected based on authentication status and role:

### Authentication-Only Protection
Some routes require any authenticated user:
- Dashboard
- Profile settings
- Basic reporting

### Role-Based Protection
Other routes require specific roles:
- **System Configuration**: `ADMINISTRADOR`, `DIRECTOR`
- **User Management**: `ADMINISTRADOR`, `DIRECTOR`
- **Advanced Analytics**: `ADMINISTRADOR`, `DIRECTOR`, `EDITOR-TM`, `EDITOR-TG`
- **Flight Dispatch**: `DESPACHADOR`, `ADMINISTRADOR`
- **Maintenance Records**: `MANTENIMIENTO`, `ADMINISTRADOR`

### Access Denied

If you attempt to access a page without sufficient permissions:
1. You'll be redirected to an "Unauthorized" page (403 error)
2. The page explains why access was denied
3. Quick action buttons help you navigate back to the Dashboard

## Logging Out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the user menu">
    Click on your user avatar or name in the top-right corner of the application.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select 'Logout'">
    Click the "Logout" or "Cerrar Sesión" option from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm logout">
    Your session will be terminated:
    - The HTTP-only cookie will be cleared
    - You'll be redirected to the login page
    - All in-memory authentication data will be removed
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Always log out when using shared computers or when you've finished your session, especially on public networks.
</Warning>

## Security Best Practices

### Password Guidelines
- Use a strong, unique password (minimum 6 characters, but longer is better)
- Include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Never share your password with others
- Change your password regularly

### Account Security
- Log out after each session on shared devices
- Don't enable "Remember Me" on public computers
- Report suspicious activity to your administrator immediately
- Review your account activity regularly

### Browser Security
- Keep your browser updated to the latest version
- Enable browser security features
- Avoid accessing SIGSAHO from untrusted networks without VPN
- Clear browser cache and cookies on shared devices

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Access the Login Page
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify the URL is correct
3. Try clearing browser cache and cookies
4. Contact IT support if the issue persists

### Session Expires Frequently
- This may be due to security policies
- Contact your administrator to review session timeout settings
- Ensure you're not accessing from multiple devices simultaneously

### Forgotten Password
1. Contact your system administrator
2. Provide your username or email for verification
3. Follow the administrator's password reset procedure

<Note>
SIGSAHO does not currently support self-service password reset. All password changes must be performed by system administrators.
</Note>
